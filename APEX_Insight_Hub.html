06 · Context

How does behaviour vary by cohort?

Multi-day use ranges from 47.1% to 88.7% across departments — a 41-point spread against the 53.9% platform average. Patterns here are context for research, not a scorecard.

Dashed red line = platform average (53.9%). Department figures are August multi-day use rates.

Evidence chain

Signal

Spread 47.1%–88.7% multi-day use across five departments.

Cross-read

The two reporting-heavy teams hold the habit base; GT2025 sits far below overall.

Hypothesis

Reporting workflows build habits; exploratory use does not.

Action

Interview GT2025 users on first-session outcome; check whether LPC is their entry workflow (UX Research).

Watch metric

Multi-day use by department, next window.
