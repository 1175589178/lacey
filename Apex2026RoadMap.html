汇报要点

01 · 改了什么

优先级与角色

优先级从“交付”转为客户价值与团队自主：Remove coordination / translation / waiting；11 个传统角色演进为 12 个 AI 增强角色；协作从 hand-off 转向 shared ownership。

02 · 怎么改

节奏与度量

Agent 嵌入产品生命周期 6 个阶段；18 项能力按 2026 Q1–Q4 排布（done → DEV/PoC → plan）；以 4 项 OKR 指标衡量，均为 On track。

03 · 底线在哪

治理与护栏

财务逻辑保持 Deterministic / Governed / Reproducible；AI 不绕过控制与治理；四项不可谈判护栏 Auditable / Explainable / Bounded / Secure；从 Assistive → Guided → Automated 渐进。

01

Remove coordination

Simplify the operating model. Teams spend less energy arranging work and more energy solving it.

Example: Scrum Master agent -> more self-orchestration team

02

Remove translation

Bring engineers closer to customers and business context, so the need is understood before a requirement is built.

03

Remove waiting

Put clear guardrails into the flow of work, enabling safe decisions without unnecessary approval queues.

Example: Rule of Five + encoding controls -> make the right decision without waiting for governance

第 07 章 / 07

Apex AI 治理与护栏

Apex AI Governance and Guardrails

定位、原则与不可谈判的护栏

约 1.5 分钟
A 组 · 定位与边界
OBJECTIVE

Build AI into Apex Platform at all levels.

Incorporate AI into the platform to enhance productivity, insight generation, and decision-making—without compromising governance, controls, or the integrity of financial results..

AI WILL

Orchestrate workflows.

Assist with analysis and interpretation.

Improve how users interact with data and models.

AI WILL NOT

Replace core financial calculations

Bypass controls or governance

OUTPUTS

No AI-generated postings, valuations, P&L, or regulatory numbers

No autonomous changes to reference data, limits, curves, mappings, or model parameters.

No direct execution of privileged actions (prod deploys, data deletes, entitlement changes) without human approval.

B 组 · 原则与用例
ALL FINANCIAL LOGIC REMAINS

Deterministic · Governed · Reproducible

AI enhances how we use the platform—it does not change how results are calculated.
TEAMS SHOULD FOCUS ON PRACTICAL, HIGH-VALUE USE CASES

Natural language interaction (e.g., “Explain IRR movement this week”)

Scenario orchestration (e.g., run and compare multiple scenarios automatically)

Insight generation (summarise key drivers, anomalies, and trends)

Productivity acceleration (assist with queries, debugging, and data exploration)

C 组 · 架构与护栏
AI SHOULD

Auditable (inputs, outputs, and decisions tracked)

Explainable (no black-box decisions for financial outcomes)

Bounded (no uncontrolled actions or direct system changes)

Secure (respect data access and security controls)

AI-ENABLED FUNCTIONALITY MUST BE

Auditable (inputs, outputs, and decisions tracked)

Explainable (no black-box decisions for financial outcomes)

Bounded (no uncontrolled actions or direct system changes)

Secure (respect data access and security controls)

DESIGN FOR

Incorrect or incomplete AI outputs

Service/tool failures

Fallback to deterministic processes

AI OUTPUTS MUST:

Be clearly distinguishable from system-calculated results

Not be treated as authoritative without validation

START SIMPLE AND CONTROLLED

Assistive → Guided → Automated (over time)

We should not attempt full automation upfront

9 个长卡片归为三组；Architecture Expectations 与 Guardrails 四点内容完全相同，合并为并排对照，不再重复出现两次。

Apex AI 治理与护栏

约 1.5 分钟

这一章回答“我们凭什么放心”：AI 增强使用方式，不改变结果如何计算。

两句话记住：AI 不产生财务数字、不绕过治理；AI 功能必须可审计、可解释、有边界、安全。

落地节奏是 Assistive → Guided → Automated，不追求一步到位的全自动。
